Advancing the Science of Dance

A bridge between the ancient wisdom of Natya Shastra and modern research methodologies.

About the Artist

Ramachandra is a dedicated Bharatanatyam artist and scholar whose work focuses on the technical precision and philosophical depth of Indian classical dance. With over two decades of practice, he explores the physiological and spiritual intersections of movement.

Research Output

Vol 1: The Physics of Mudra

A comprehensive study on the pressure points and neurological impact of hand gestures in classical dance forms.

Vol 2: Kinetic Geometry

Analyzing the spatial mathematical patterns found in the 108 Karanas as described in ancient texts.

Vol 3: Rhythmic Structures

An investigation into the complex time signatures of Carnatic music and their translation into footwork.

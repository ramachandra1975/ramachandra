Naredla Rama Chandra

Consciousness Studies VOL. I

Complete Science of Consciousness
Ancient Knowledge vs Artificial Intelligence
Flagship Work

This landmark work presents a comprehensive framework comparing the ancient Vedic science of consciousness with modern artificial intelligence paradigms. Drawing from thousands of years of accumulated wisdom encoded in Sanskrit texts, the author demonstrates how ancient seers mapped the full spectrum of human consciousness with a precision that modern neuroscience is only beginning to approach. The work challenges the reductionist view of mind and offers a unified theory of consciousness rooted in Vedic epistemology.

“Ancient seers mapped the cosmos of consciousness with a precision that modern science is only beginning to rediscover.”
— Naredla Rama Chandra

Key Themes

  • Vedic models of consciousness vs. computational models
  • The role of Prana and Kundalini in neural activation
  • Sanskrit as a programming language for the mind
  • Comparison of AI neural networks with Vedic chakras

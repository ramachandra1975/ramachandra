The 108 Karanas: A Research Overview

The 108 Karanas, as detailed in the Natya Shastra, serve as the fundamental kinetic units of Indian classical tradition. My research redefines these movements as a Master Consciousness Protocol. By exploring the intersection of neuro-geometry and bio-acoustics, we observe how these transitions align human physiology with cosmic frequencies. This work bridges ancient movement science with modern astrophysical concepts, revealing a "Cosmic Choreography" that influences both mental clarity and cellular health.

Core Research Publications

01. Master Consciousness Protocol (MCP)

A 300-page foundational volume establishing a multi-disciplinary framework for studying Ragas and their physiological effects. It includes the 365-day Daily Raga Calendar, mapping sounds to chakras and endocrine systems for clinical wellness.

02. Sapta Swaras & Healing Science

An exploration of the biochemical impact of Carnatic music. This work maps the 22 Shrutis to the human nervous system, establishing Raga Chikitsa (sound medicine) as a precise tool for modern neuro-immunology.

03. The Cosmic Choreography

A bridge between the 108 Karanas and Astrophysics. This book interprets ancient dance transitions as geometric alignments that tune the human spine to the Earth's electromagnetic resonance.

04. Sampoorna Manovijnana Sangraha

A comprehensive psychological treatise synthesizing ancient Indic philosophy with modern cognitive science, focusing on Citta, Manas, and neuroplasticity through 240 academic case studies.

05. Yoga: The Eternal Science (Vols I & II)

Tracing the architecture of the Vedic-Yogic tradition from the Upanishads to the Natya Shastra, exploring the Guru-Shishya Parampara and the neuroscience of Samadhi.

06. Beeja Akshara & DNA Resonance

Investigating how Sanskrit phonemes and Matrikas (seed sounds) act as vibrational keys to unlock genetic potential through the ancient practice of Nyasa.

07. Vedic Bio-Acoustics

Connecting the Gandharva Veda with contemporary psycho-neuro-immunology, this volume provides a scientific basis for Nada Yoga as a clinical healing modality.

08. Cosmic Vak: The Story of Language

A cosmological history of communication, detailing the four stages of sound (Para to Vaikhari) and how eternal vibrations manifest as human language and time.
